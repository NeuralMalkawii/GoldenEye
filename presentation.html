01 · Brief Introduction 02 / 11 · WHAT IS GOLDENEYE
◆ The system

An aerial human detector,
built specifically for the desert.

GoldenEye takes a frame from a UAV camera, locates every person in it, and streams annotated results to a command-centre interface — in a browser, or fully offline on a Pi 5 with a Hailo-8L accelerator. No cloud round-trip, no GPS dependency, no proprietary SDK.

01 · Edge-capable
Offline by default

Pi 5 + Hailo-8L AI Kit. ≥ 30 FPS target at 1080p on an INT8-quantised ONNX export.

02 · 4K-aware
SAHI tiling

Median target on 4K Shaheen imagery is 68 px. Tiled inference keeps targets above the model's receptive field.

03 · Real-time
WS live stream

Bidirectional WebSocket with back-pressure (drop oldest, max 4 queued). ByteTrack-style IDs persist across frames.

04 · Open
Reproducible

Model card on HF Hub, Docker compose, 66 pytest tests, CI to Vercel + Railway, MIT licence.

02 · Motivation 03 / 11 · WHY SAR DETECTION
◆ The desert is a hostile detector

Every minute matters,
and every degree of contrast hides a body.

~96hours
Median survival in 45°C heat
For an adult without water in UAE / Wadi Rum summer conditions. Every hour after disappearance squares the radius the team must sweep.
3,000+km²
Typical UAE Empty-Quarter sweep area
A single trained dog handler + spotter covers ~10 km²/day. A 4K UAV at 95 m altitude covers ~80× more.
68px
Median target size on Shaheen 4K
A standing person at 50 m altitude is roughly 68×67 px in a 3840×2160 frame. After a single 640-letterbox that's 11 px — below most detectors' receptive field.
FRAMING FORM GE-MOT/01

Shaheen (AUS) solved the easy half of SAR detection — UAE desert, low altitude, clear sky. We start from there and ask the harder operational questions: does it still work in a different country, from higher up, through a degraded sensor, on a battery-powered Pi?

None of those questions can be answered with another accuracy number. They need an altitude curve, a degradation curve, a cross-environment matrix — and a deployable system to run them on.

◆ Operational consequence
A 12-second false-negative on a UAV pass
is the difference between a rescue and a recovery.

02 · Problem Statement 04 / 11 · THREE FAILURE MODES
◆ What current SAR detection gets wrong

Three failure modes,
three things we had to solve.

PROBLEM-01 SMALL OBJECT
Targets too small for the receptive field

A 640-letterbox of a 4K aerial frame shrinks the median person to ~11×11 px. That's below where YOLOv8n can reliably activate anchors.

PROBLEM-02 SENSOR DEGRADATION
Models trained clean break in the field

Motion blur, JPEG compression, heat-haze and glare strip ~12pp from a model that was never trained against them.

PROBLEM-03 EDGE DEPLOYMENT
No cloud, no GPS, no warm room

A field UAV operates without LTE, on a 5–8 W power budget, at temperatures that throttle most accelerators. The target is a Pi 5 + Hailo-8L AI Kit.

06 · Challenges & Limitations 10 / 11 · WHAT WE'D DO DIFFERENTLY
◆ Three honest framings

What broke, what's still hard,
and what's next.

CH-01 WHAT BROKE
We had to discard 2 of the 4 stages.
  • ▸ Synth pseudo-labels are noisy. Grounding-DINO teacher on the "with-human" synthetic split agreed with manual review only ~78% of the time. We weighted those samples at 0.2× in the mixed loss.
  • ▸ UE5 + AirSim was deferred. The photoreal simulation rig didn't fit in the timeline. We evaluated entirely on real Shaheen test imagery instead, which the panel can rerun directly.
  • ▸ Wadi Rum field trip never happened. So Wadi Rum is the matrix-blank we honestly can't fill in. Our cross-domain story is HERIDAL forest, not red sand.
CH-02 WHAT'S STILL HARD
The 80m+ ceiling, and shadow occlusion.
  • ▸ Above 80 m altitude, even SAHI loses recall on figures crouched/prone. The receptive field is fighting physics.
  • ▸ Shadows confuse the head. Top failure mode in NB13 was "person in shadow under a vehicle" — Shaheen real_data has very few of these.
  • ▸ Tracker drift at low frame rates. Below 4 FPS sustained the IoU tracker reassigns IDs. ByteTrack with motion model would help — wasn't worth the dep.
  • ▸ Confidence is poorly calibrated. 0.9 ≠ 90% precision empirically — we ship temperature scaling but haven't fit T on a held-out set.
CH-03 WHAT'S NEXT
A red-sand dataset, and the Pi in a field box.
  • ▸ Wadi Rum field collection — 2 days of DJI footage with consenting figures at 6 altitudes. Closes the matrix-blank above.
  • ▸ Pi 5 + Hailo enclosure. 3D-printed mount, M.2 HAT+, real outdoor 60-min run with a thermal log.
  • ▸ Mission map → KML pipeline. Already wired; needs polygon-to-waypoint conversion for actual drone flight planning.
  • ▸ Active-learning loop. Low-confidence detections (0.3–0.5) flagged in the backend for offline labelling; feed back into training v1.1.
  • ▸ Auto-PDF mission report. When a video job finishes, generate a one-page PDF brief for ground teams.
